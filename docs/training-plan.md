### Race loads (Men's Open and Doubles, verified on hyrox.com 7 Nov 2026)

Weights are in pounds, with the official kilograms in brackets. Doubles uses the same loads as singles. Every sled load is the **added plate weight**, counting the sled as 0, so training is slightly heavier than racing.

| # | Station | Race load |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | SkiErg | 1000 m |
| 2 | Sled push | 50 m, 335 lb (152 kg) |
| 3 | Sled pull | 50 m, 227 lb (103 kg) |
| 4 | Burpee broad jumps | 80 m |
| 5 | Row | 1000 m |
| 6 | Farmers carry | 200 m, 2 × 53 lb (24 kg) |
| 7 | Sandbag lunges | 100 m, 44 lb (20 kg) |
| 8 | Wall balls | 100 reps, 13 lb (6 kg) ball |

Every Station is preceded by a 1 km run: 8 km of running in total. Running is about half of a PRO race and nearer 60% of a recreational one, and PRO men slow about 15% from run 1 to run 8 (research note, §1).

| Build | 6–15 | Dec 14 – Feb 21 | Station volume climbs to race amounts, Compromised intervals begin, long run grows |

| Taper | 22–23 | Apr 5 – Apr 18 | Volume −40% then −60%; intensity and frequency kept |
| Race week | 24 | Apr 19 – Apr 25 | Shakeout, rest, **race Thu Apr 22** |

**Deload weeks:** 5, 10, 15, 20. Volume drops by about a third, Race load and paces stay, no Key session, Station targets off. Readiness can call for an extra one: three Readiness flags in a Week → the Coach suggests the rest of that Week a third lighter.

**Compromised run tests:** Weeks 2, 9, 14, 19. **Race simulations:** Weeks 16, 18, 21.

## How a Week works

- Each Week is a **Bucket**: Strength, HYROX B, Run, HYROX A, plus one Optional Easy run (two from Build, see below). Do them on any day, in any order, inside Monday–Sunday. The Bucket order is the order the Coach suggests when nothing else decides: Strength first on fresh legs, HYROX B (ergs, jumps, ball) the day after, the Run two days clear of Strength, HYROX A (sleds, carries, lunges) last on the freshest day, with the Easy run at the weekend.
- Every required Session fits the **Session cap** of 45 minutes, Set up excluded. Only a Race simulation runs longer.
- The Coach picks **Up next** each day: no two Hard sessions on consecutive days when avoidable; never Run the day after Strength; Strength at least 3 hours from any run, and first when they share a day. In Catch-up these are warnings, not blocks (ADR-0007).
- A required Session not done by Sunday is **Late**, not missed: Up next comes from the oldest Week with a Late session until it is caught up. Each Session keeps its own Week's Prescription. The Optional sessions never wait. 7 or more days with no Done or Partial Session → the Coach suggests the rest of the Week Up next comes from a third lighter (never a Deload week or the Taper).
- Holidays (Fri Dec 25, Fri Jan 1) need no special handling: the Bucket just gets done on other days.

## Progression

- **Stations** are always at Race load. Progression is **Station volume** first (the table below), then time: each Station shows its **Station target**, the Station best at that volume, or the best less about 3% once the last two Sessions logged at that volume came in at RPE 7 or under. The best can come from any round; the two logs are two Sessions, so a Station done twice in one Session counts once. Row, sandbag lunges, burpee broad jumps and SkiErg reach race volume first: they separate finishers most, and sled pull and lunges punish slower athletes hardest (research note, §1).
- **Lifts** progress by load from RPE: all target reps at RPE ≤ 7 → +5 lb on squat and RDL, +5% on calves and carries (rounded to the nearest 5 lb, at least +5 lb) next time. RPE ≥ 9 or missed reps → hold. Two holds in a row → −5%, rounded to the nearest 5 lb. Smallest step 5 lb.
- **Runs** progress by the Training paces, which come from the latest Compromised run test.
- The volume-then-time progression at fixed load has no trial behind it (research note, §5); every Session's Training load is logged so the Program can be judged after Race day.

## Session templates

Warm-ups and cooldowns are included in the minutes. Set up is not: it is untimed and shown as "+ ~5′ setup" beside the minutes.

### HYROX A and HYROX B (~35–40 min)

| Block | Prescription | Min |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up | Easy jog | 5 |
| Set up | Every Station the Session uses, at Race load. Set up once; they stay set up between rounds | ~5, not counted |
| Rounds | 8 or 4 × (Station at its Station volume → run @R, RPE 7–8), race order, straight on with no rest. Each Station shows its Station target | calculated |
| Cooldown | Walk, relaxed breathing; no extra finisher | 4 |

The minutes are calculated, not set: warm-up and cooldown, each run's meters at the athlete's Race run pace, and each Station's Station estimate (its Station best at that volume, else a best at another volume scaled by share, else a default from typical Men's Open splits), rounded to the nearest 5. Set up is left out. They move with each Compromised run test and each new Station best; the 45-minute Session cap is a ceiling, not a target. Before any test or log, at @R 5:15/km and the defaults:

| | Foundation (1–5) | Build early (6–10) | Build late (11–15) | Race specific (16–21) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HYROX A | 35 | 40 | 40 | 45 |
| HYROX B | 35 | 45 | 45 | 40 |
| Deload | 30 (Week 5) | 35 (Week 10) | 35 (Week 15) | 30 A · 25 B (Week 20) |

- **HYROX A**: sled push, sled pull, farmers carry, sandbag lunges. **HYROX B**: SkiErg, burpee broad jumps, row, wall balls. Wall balls always last: it is where heart rate, lactate and RPE peak.
- The run comes **after** the Station, so every run is a Compromised run, at Race run pace (@R), RPE 7–8. Week 1 uses the Starting point's estimate until the baseline test.
- **Continuous**: each round follows the last with no prescribed rest, the join between the two passes through the Stations included. The athlete recovers by easing a run; that run is logged as it was run, and counts like any other.
- Log each run's split; the Coach shows the Fade from the first run to the last (run 1 against run 8 in an 8-round Session).
- Deload weeks drop the Station the athlete did best last time and keep Race load, with no Station targets: Weeks 5 and 10 go through the other three Stations twice (6 rounds), Weeks 15 and 20 once (3 rounds).
- Technique before speed in Foundation; the Station targets start counting once a Station has two logs at its volume.

### Run (~45 min)

Alternates between two forms from Build on; Foundation is threshold only. Both are long intervals: reps of 2 minutes or more and at least 15 minutes of hard work, the dose that beats continuous running (research note, §2).

| Form | Block | Prescription | Min |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Threshold | Warm-up | Easy jog, conversational | 10 |
| | Intervals | Week-specific @ Threshold pace, RPE 6–7, never racing, 90″ jog between | 21–28 |
| | Cooldown | Easy jog | 7 |
| Compromised intervals | Warm-up | Easy jog | 7–10 |
| | Intervals | 4–5 × (1 km @ Race run pace, then 20 wall balls at 13 lb), 60″ rest | 26–32 |
| | Cooldown | Easy jog | 5–7 |

Substitution for the wall balls when no ball is at hand: 20 air squats + 10 burpees. Deload weeks replace the Run with 35 minutes easy. On a treadmill the intervals run there and the wall balls happen beside it.

### Compromised run test (~45 min; Weeks 2, 9, 14, 19)

Warm-up 8 min easy + 2 strides → **4 × (1 km run at full effort → Station at Race load, half race volume)** in race order: SkiErg 500 m, sled push 25 m, row 500 m, wall balls 50 → 4 min cooldown. Replaces that Week's Run session. Indoor: treadmill at 1% incline. The average of the four run splits is the **Race run pace**; the first split is open running and the gap to the fourth is the Fade.

**Training paces** from Race run pace: Threshold pace = Race run pace − 15 s/km; Easy pace = Race run pace + 60 to 90 s/km. These offsets are the coach's judgment, not a published model; review them after the Week 2 test. Before the first test, the Starting point's estimated 1 km split stands in.

### Strength (~45 min)

High-load compound lifts progressed by RPE: the kind of strength work that improves running economy; light and isometric work does not (research note, §3).

| Block | Exercise | Weeks 1–15 | Weeks 16–21 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up | Bike + movement prep + ramp-up sets | 6 min | 6 min |
| Strength | Back squat | 3 × 5 @ RPE 7, 90–120 s rest | 2 × 4 @ RPE 7–8 |
| Strength | Romanian deadlift | 3 × 6 @ RPE 7, slow lowering | 2 × 4 @ RPE 7–8 |
| Unilateral | Bulgarian split squat | 2 × 6 / leg @ RPE 7 | dropped |
| Carry | Heavy farmers carry, dumbbells, heavier than Race load when possible | 3 × 40 m | 2 × 40 m |
| Tendon | Single-leg calf raise off a step, dumbbell in hand | 3 × 8 / leg, 2–3 s lowering | same |
| Tendon | Seated calf raise, dumbbell or barbell on knees | 2 × 10 | same |

- **Week 1 is calibration:** ramp each lift to RPE 7 to find its Working weight.
- **Deload:** 2 sets of each main lift at ~85–90% Working weight, RPE ≤ 6.
- Lift at least 3 hours away from any run, and first when they share a day; concurrent training costs explosive strength most when the two are in one session (research note, §3).

### Easy run (Optional)

Easy pace, RPE 2–4, full sentences. No tempo, no hills. Grows into a 60-minute long run by Week 17 (minutes in the tables below). A **second Easy run** of 25–30 minutes is offered from Build on, in any Week that follows a Week whose four required Sessions were all Done. Easy volume is the first thing to cut when a Week is short: hard Sessions kept, easy volume cut, costs little over a few weeks (research note, §6).

### Race simulation (exempt from the Session cap)

Race order, Race load, conservative first run, RPE 8 (not all-out). Replaces that Week's HYROX A. Log every run split: the Fade from run 1 to the last run is the number to watch. Done with the partner when possible, each taking half of every Station, as Doubles practice.

| Week | Simulation | Content | Min |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | Half simulation A | 4 × (1 km run + Station 1–4: SkiErg, sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps) at full volume | 60–70 |
| 18 | Half simulation B | 4 × (1 km run + Station 5–8: row, farmers carry, sandbag lunges, wall balls) at full volume | 60–70 |
| 21 | Full-run simulation | 8 × (1 km run + Station 1–8 at **half race volume**): SkiErg 500 m, sled push 25 m, sled pull 25 m, BBJ 40 m, row 500 m, farmers 100 m, lunges 50 m, wall balls 50 | 75–85 |

## Paces and treadmill

- Training paces come from the latest Compromised run test: Race run pace, Threshold pace (−15 s/km), Easy pace (+60 to +90 s/km). Example: an average split of 5:30/km (10.9 km/h) gives a Threshold pace of about 5:15/km (11.4 km/h) and an Easy pace of 6:30–7:00/km (9.2–8.6 km/h). The app computes exact values.
- Indoor runs show **speed + incline** (default 1%, km/h with an mph option). The treadmill console distance is the truth, not the watch.

## Projected finish

Projected finish = sum of the eight Station bests at full Station volume + 8 × Race run pace + 6 minutes of transitions, shown as a projection. Its Doubles share takes half of the Station part. Progress shows it first, then the Station bests, Race run pace from the tests, and Training load.

## Operating rules

1. RPE 7 ≈ 3 reps in reserve; RPE 8 ≈ 2.
2. Pain ≥ 3 or rising the next morning → no burpee broad jumps or sled push that day (row or SkiErg instead), interval reps −30%. Pain ≥ 5 → easy/low-impact Session or rest.
3. Sleep under 6 h → one rep fewer, one round fewer, and an RPE cap.
4. Station targets are to beat, not to chase at the cost of form; technique before speed.
5. Threshold reps are never raced.
6. Finish every Hard session fresh enough for the next one.
