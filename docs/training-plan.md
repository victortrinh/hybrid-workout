## How a Week works

- Each Week is a **Bucket**: Speed, Strength, Threshold, HYROX, plus an Optional Easy run. Do them on any day, in any order, inside Monday–Sunday.
- The Coach picks **Up next** each day: no two Hard sessions on consecutive days when avoidable; never Speed the day after HYROX or Strength; Strength + Easy run on the same day is fine.
- The **Key session** can carry over up to 3 days into the next Week. Anything else not done by Sunday is Skipped. Two or more Skipped → the Coach suggests a lighter restart week.
- Holidays (Fri Dec 25, Fri Jan 1) need no special handling: the Bucket just gets done on other days.

## Session templates

Warm-ups and cooldowns are included in the minutes.

### Speed (~45 min)

| Block | Prescription | Min |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up | Easy jog + leg swings, skips, drills | 10 |
| Plyometrics | Pogo jumps 2 × 15 contacts; broad jumps 3 × 3 (full reset) | 5 |
| Accelerations | 15 m × 2–4, walk back. Start 80–90%, build to 90–95% over weeks | 5 |
| Intervals | Week-specific (table below), ~20 min block | 20 |
| Cooldown | Jog / walk | 5 |

Substitutions: no floor space or Readiness flag → **sled push sprints 10–15 m (light)** or **bike sprints 8–10 s**. On a treadmill, only the intervals run there; jumps and accelerations stay on the floor.

### Strength (~45 min)

| Block | Exercise | Weeks 1–14 | Weeks 15–25 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up | Bike + movement prep + ramp-up sets | 6 min | 6 min |
| Strength | Back squat | 3 × 5 @ RPE 7, 90–120 s rest | 2 × 4 @ RPE 7–8 |
| Strength | Romanian deadlift | 3 × 6 @ RPE 7, slow lowering | 2 × 4 @ RPE 7–8 |
| Unilateral | Bulgarian split squat | 2 × 6 / leg @ RPE 7 | 2 × 6 / leg @ RPE 7 |
| Tendon | Single-leg calf raise off a step, dumbbell in hand | 3 × 8 / leg, 2–3 s lowering | same |
| Tendon | Seated calf raise, dumbbell or barbell on knees | 2 × 10 | same |
| Upper | Pull-ups + push-ups superset | 2 × 6–10 each | same |

- **Week 1 is calibration:** ramp each lift to RPE 7 to find its Working weight.
- **Progression:** all target reps at RPE ≤ 7 → +5 lb on squat/RDL, +5% on calves (rounded to the nearest 5 lb, at least +5 lb) next time. RPE ≥ 9 or missed reps → hold. Two holds in a row → −5%, rounded to the nearest 5 lb.
- **Units:** every load is in pounds; the smallest step is 5 lb.
- **Deload:** 2 sets of each main lift at ~85–90% Working weight, RPE ≤ 6.

### Threshold (~45 min)

| Block | Prescription | Min |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up | Easy jog, conversational | 10 |
| Threshold | Week-specific (table below) @ Threshold pace, RPE 6–7, never racing | 21–28 |
| Easy + cooldown | Easy jog | 7–14 |

Deload weeks replace this with 35–40 min easy running.

### HYROX (~45–50 min)

| Block | Prescription | Min |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up | Easy jog + lunges + Station prep (check sled and erg setup) | 7 |
| Circuit | 3 rounds of run + Stations (by phase, below) | 33–38 |
| Cooldown | Walk, relaxed breathing; no extra finisher | 5 |

Stations alternate weekly between **Rotation A** (sled push, sled pull, farmers carry, sandbag lunges) in odd weeks and **Rotation B** (SkiErg, row, burpee broad jumps, wall balls) in even weeks. Technique before speed.

| Phase | Each round | Station volume per round | Load |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | 600 m run + 2 Stations | Sled push/pull 25 m, farmers 50 m, lunges 25 m · Ski/row 250 m, BBJ 10 m, wall balls 20 | ~60% Race load |
| 5K development | 700 m run + 2–3 Stations | Sled push/pull 25 m, farmers 100 m, lunges 50 m · Ski/row 300 m, BBJ 20 m, wall balls 30 | 70–80% |
| HYROX build | 800 m run + 1–2 Stations | Sled push/pull 50 m, farmers 100 m, lunges 50 m · Ski/row 500 m, BBJ 40 m, wall balls 50 | 90–100% |
| Race specific | 1 km run + 1 Station | Full race volume of that Station | 100% |

Wall balls always use the 13 lb (6 kg) ball; volume scales instead of load. Station loads are rounded to the nearest 5 lb.

### Easy run (Optional)

Easy pace, RPE 2–4, full sentences. No tempo, no hills. Grows into a 60-minute long run by week 23 (minutes in the table below).

### 5K test (~45 min)

Warm-up 12 min easy + 4 × 15 s strides → **5 km all-out** → 8 min cooldown. Replaces that Week's Speed session. Indoor: treadmill at 1% incline. Each result records its Environment and resets the Training paces.

### Race simulation (60–75 min; week 24 ~75–85 min)

Race order, Race load, conservative first run, RPE 8 (not all-out). Replaces that Week's HYROX session.

| Week | Simulation | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | Half simulation A | 4 × (1 km run + Station 1–4: SkiErg, sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps) |
| 21 | Half simulation B | 4 × (1 km run + Station 5–8: row, farmers carry, sandbag lunges, wall balls) |
| 24 | Full-run simulation | 8 × (1 km run + Station 1–8 at **half race volume**), race order, Race load: SkiErg 500 m, sled push 25 m, sled pull 25 m, BBJ 40 m, row 500 m, farmers 100 m, lunges 50 m, wall balls 50 |

## Paces and treadmill

- Training paces come from the latest 5K test (Daniels VDOT): 5K pace, Threshold pace, Easy pace. Example: a 23:00 test gives about 4:36/km 5K pace (13.0 km/h), ~4:55/km threshold (12.2 km/h), ~5:50–6:30/km easy (10.3–9.2 km/h). The app computes exact values.
- Indoor runs show **speed + incline** (default 1%, km/h with an mph option). The treadmill console distance is the truth, not the watch.

## Operating rules

1. RPE 7 ≈ 3 reps in reserve; RPE 8 ≈ 2.
2. Pain ≥ 3 or rising the next morning → no jumps or accelerations (bike instead), interval reps −30%. Pain ≥ 5 → easy/low-impact Session or rest.
3. Sleep under 6 h → one rep fewer and an RPE cap.
4. Accelerations start at 80–90%, never maximal early; add speed gradually.
5. Threshold reps are never raced.
6. Finish Speed sessions fresh enough for the next Hard session.
