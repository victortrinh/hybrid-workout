# Bucket order: Strength first, HYROX A last, so a plain week lands on the athlete's days

The Bucket order decides Up next whenever nothing else does: no Session picked, nothing Late, no rest suggested. Until now it was HYROX A, HYROX B, Run, Strength, so a Week with nothing logged opened on HYROX A every Monday, and Strength, the only Session the spacing rules keep the Run away from, came last, where it kept landing on the weekend or the day before the Run.

The athlete trains Monday to Friday and keeps the weekend for the Easy run or rest (decided 8 November 2026). Laid out on those days, the Week that works is: Strength on Monday on fresh legs, HYROX B on Tuesday (SkiErg, burpee broad jumps, row, wall balls: little leg loading the day after squats), the Run on Wednesday, two days clear of Strength, a rest on Thursday, HYROX A on Friday (sleds, farmers carry, sandbag lunges, and the 1 km runs in Race specific) on the freshest day with the weekend to recover, and the Easy run on Saturday.

So the Bucket is now **Strength, HYROX B, Run, HYROX A, then the Optional session(s)**, and the Program, Today, Plan and Progress show it in that order. Nothing else changes: the Week is still a Bucket done on any day and in any order, the spacing rules still hold (they will suggest rest on the Tuesday and the Wednesday of a Monday–Tuesday–Wednesday block, and the athlete taps past them), Catch-up still serves Late sessions oldest first and in Bucket order within a Week, and the Race simulations keep HYROX A's slot, now last among the required Sessions.

- **Why not pin Sessions to weekdays?** The athlete's days move, and a pinned day would make every slipped Session a special case. The order gets the usual week right and costs nothing when it isn't usual.
- **Why HYROX B before HYROX A?** The day after Strength is the one to spare the legs on; A is the sled-and-lunge Session, and in Race specific its runs are the 1 km ones. B's four Stations also take about twice as long as A's, so B is the better Session to have behind you before the Run.
- **Why does the activity matcher still rank HYROX ahead of Strength?** Candidates rank by heart rate and duration first; Bucket order only breaks ties, so a watch circuit still asks HYROX B, HYROX A, then Strength.

What this gives up: the Monday Hard session is now the one the Run must keep two days from, so an athlete who starts a Week late (Wednesday, say) sees a rest suggested on Thursday more often than before. That is the spacing rule doing its job; the Do it anyway button covers the rest.
