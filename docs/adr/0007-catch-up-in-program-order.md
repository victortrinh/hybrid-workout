# Catch-up: a late Session waits in Program order instead of being missed

Until now the calendar decided everything. Up next came only from the Week holding today, a Week's undone Sessions became Coach-Skipped misses on its Sunday (the Key session three days later), and two misses made the next Week a lighter re-entry. A busy week meant training was silently thrown away, while the athlete would rather do one Session after the other and catch up on their own.

So while a Week that is over still holds a required Session with no log, the Coach is in Catch-up. Up next comes from the oldest such Week, picked among its Late sessions by the usual rules, and the next Week only starts once that one is cleared. Today shows the Late sessions first in the Bucket, then the current Week, whose Sessions can still be picked instead, and a quiet count of how far behind the athlete is ("2 Sessions behind · Week 1"), with no nudges. Once nothing is late, the calendar Weeks work exactly as before.

- **Only required Sessions wait.** The Optional session from an over Week drops away, as before. A Session logged Skipped is the athlete's own "not doing this one" and lets the pointer move past it.
- **Each Session keeps its own Week's Prescription.** A Week 3 Session done during the Week 4 Deload gets Week 3's full Prescription, so the Deload comes after the work it recovers from. Working weights still move with logged sets, whatever the date.
- **The spacing rules become warnings.** Catching up means fitting more than a Week's Sessions into one, so "no Hard sessions back to back" and "no Speed after Strength or HYROX" no longer give a rest day: Today says the rest is suggested and the athlete can do the Session anyway.
- **No more carry-over.** The Key session's three days are covered by Catch-up, so the carry-over rule and its "carried until" date go.
- **The lighter re-entry Week follows a gap in training, not misses.** With nothing ever missed, it is suggested after 7 or more days with no Done or Partial log, for the rest of the Week Up next comes from, still as a Suggestion, and never in a Deload week or the Taper.
- **Watch workouts match Late sessions too**, since they're in the Bucket.
- **Race day doesn't move.** On the first Monday of Race specific the calendar takes over again: Sessions still late from earlier Weeks become Coach-Skipped, and Today says so once ("3 Sessions from Weeks 14–15 dropped to keep Race specific on schedule"). Without that anchor the athlete could reach Race day mid-build, with no Taper.

We considered walking the whole Program in order with no calendar at all. It breaks on Race day, and it would have meant re-deciding Deload timing, Week pages and every rule that reads "this Week". We also considered keeping the spacing rules strict while behind; that only slows catching up, and the athlete said the pace is theirs to judge.

What this gives up: the Program can now run behind the calendar until Race specific, and nothing forces it back sooner. The re-entry Week no longer notices a Week of Sessions skipped one by one while training goes on.
